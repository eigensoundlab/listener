## Sources and what was assumed

- **Temporal integration, 1 kHz tone, where it is largest.** dB the 5 ms tone lies above the equally loud 200 ms tone, the 5 ms tone held at 56 dB SPL. Florentine, Buus & Poulsen (1996), JASA 99(3):1633-1644; 6 listeners, adaptive two-interval matching: a peak of 18 to 19 dB when the 5 ms tone is about 56 dB SPL [established]. Read: abstract (journal and 1995 meeting); the tones' gating from Buus, Florentine & Poulsen (1999), which says the 1996 study used the same; the paper itself is behind a paywall and was not read. Assumed or noted: later studies of the same laboratory with a roving-level procedure found maxima of 26 to 27 dB (Buus et al. 1999).
